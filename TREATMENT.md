# What Happened to Flint, Michigan? The Town GM Built

≈ 20 min (length follows the material) · warm archive investigation, house rules (dark warm ground, sepia archive layer,
pictures ≥ half the frame, people and paper quote cards, red only for dates and numbers, gentle score, 47 px captions,
no blank frames) · English · narrator: AI33 Pro / ElevenLabs "Storyteller Oxley" · 1920×1080, 30 fps

## The question

Flint is where American factory workers won: 44 days sitting inside General Motors' plants in the winter of 1936–37,
until the biggest carmaker in the world recognised their union. Then the town GM built lost more than half its people.
What did winning buy, and what couldn't it protect?

## Three structures considered

1. **Five acts in order** (as Kannapolis and Newton). Clear, but the series would start to feel like one template.
2. **A single clock from the first night**: the 44 days told minute by minute, the century after as an epilogue. Strong
   drama, but the decline, the water and today get squeezed into a postscript.
3. **Six dated days** (the plan's shape): each day one scene, opened by a big red date card, told in the present tense,
   each ending on the question the next day answers; the cold open starts at a beginning (the first night), not the end.

**Chosen: 3.** It gives the series a second shape, lets the strike be told as a victory, and lets the later days carry
the reckoning. Links between days are in the past tense; inside a day, the present.

## Look and sound

As Newton: warm sepia archive, paper cards for every quote, red date cards that fill the frame for a beat before each day.
Real moving image for the factory (Master Hands, Chevrolet, Flint, 1936; public domain), used large with a slow push.
Sanborn maps of 1886 and 1909 as the town's clock. The Guard in 1937 and the Guard in 2016, paired. Gentle score
(piano, harp, soft strings, clarinet; a horn for the strikers), thinning to piano for Days 5 and 6.

## Rules

- Only Flint pictures (no Detroit pictures labelled Flint); Library of Congress copies, not Getty's; no Roger & Me clips.
- Day 6 states facts and names who decided; no villain, no shock.
- GM's reasons and the workers' views side by side.
